The Math Concept: The Power Operator
To find the square of a number in Python, you do not use a standard multiplication sign (```*```) or a caret (```^```). You use the double asterisk (```**```), which is the power operator.
- ```4 ** 2``` means $4^2$ (4 multiplied by itself: 4 * 4 = 16).
- ```4 ** 3``` means $4^3$ (4 * 4 * 4 = 64).
